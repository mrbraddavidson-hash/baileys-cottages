Muskellunge (Muskie)

Esox masquinongy • Up to 50+ Inches in Moira Lake

Known as the "fish of 10,000 casts," Moira Lake delivers one of the highest muskie encounter rates in eastern Ontario. Giant specimens roam the deep channels, eastern weedline drop-offs, and island shelves. Bailey's strongly supports catch-and-release to preserve this world-class trophy fishery.

FMZ 18 Regulations & Playbook
Minimum Size:44 Inches (112 cm)
Daily Limit:1 Fish (Sport / Conserv.)
Target Depths:12 to 24 ft Weed Edges
Prime Lures:Bulldawgs, #10 Bucktails, Believers
Prime Hotspots:Papoose Channel, Plum Point Drop

TABLE FARE FAVORITE 2nd Sat in May to End of Feb 4 Authentic Catches

Walleye (Yellow Pickerel)

Sander vitreus • 16" to 26" Quality Class

Prized across Ontario for its delicate sweet meat and exhilarating evening feeding binges. Walleye stage along deep rock humps and mud flats during the day, moving onto gravel shelves and rocky points 30 minutes before sunset.

FMZ 18 Regulations & Playbook
Sport Limit:4 Fish (Conservation: 2)
Slot Protection:None between 40-53 cm (16-21 in)
Target Depths:15 to 30 ft Humps & Drop-offs
Prime Lures:1/4 oz Jigs w/ Minnow/Leech, Crawler Harness
Prime Hotspots:Stony Island Reef, North Deep Basin Edge

ACROBATIC REEF & PAD BRAWLERS 3rd Sat in June to Dec 15 5 Authentic Catches

Largemouth & Smallmouth Bass

Micropterus salmoides & dolomieu • High Density 3-5 lbs

Moira Lake boasts phenomenal dual-bass action. Aggressive largemouth thrive in the dense lilypad bays and dock structures directly outside Bailey’s Cottages, while hard-fighting bronze smallmouth dominate the offshore glacial boulder humps.

FMZ 18 Regulations & Playbook
Sport Limit:6 Fish combined (Conserv: 2)
Season Dates:3rd Sat in June to Dec 15
Target Depths:Largemouth: 2-8 ft • Smallmouth: 8-18 ft
Prime Lures:Ned Rigs, Drop Shots, Frogs, Senkos
Prime Hotspots:Bailey's Bay Pads, Green Island Shallows

Northern Pike

Esox lucius • Up to 40+ Inches in Moira Lake

Fierce, aggressive predators that prowl the cabbage weedbeds and river mouth flats. Pike provide non-stop high-energy action on hardware throughout spring, summer, and ice-up.

FMZ 18 Regulations & Playbook
Sport Limit:6 Fish (Conservation: 2)
Size Restriction:Not more than 1 over 61 cm (24 in)
Target Depths:4 to 15 ft Cabbage Weedlines
Prime Lures:Daredevle Spoons, Mepps #5, Chatterbaits
Prime Hotspots:North Marsh Inflow, River Delta, Island Flats

Pomoxis nigromaculatus & Lepomis • Family Dock Fishing

Spectacular non-stop action for kids, beginners, and spring crappie enthusiasts right off Bailey's private launch slips, wooden docks, and shoreline pilings. Delivers sweet panfish fillets and unforgettable childhood memories.

Beyond The Lake

Curated Madoc Field Guide

Just 5 to 10 minutes from your cabin deck lies downtown Madoc and Centre Hastings, home to historic bakeries, pioneer conservation areas, and adventure trails.

Hidden Goldmine Bakery

Downtown Madoc • 8 Mins Away

A must-stop local staple! Legendary for freshly baked butter tarts, artisanal sourdough, warm donuts, sandwiches, and premium espresso coffee.

Local Tip: Order butter tarts early before noon
Location: 13-17 Durham St S, Madoc

O'Hara Mill Homestead

Conservation Area • 12 Mins Away

Step back into the 1850s. Features the only operational sawmill of its kind in Ontario, picturesque covered footbridge, historic schoolhouse, and walking trails along the mill pond.

Best For: Scenic trail walks & photography
Admission: By donation • Open grounds

Heritage Trail Corridor

Hastings County • Direct Access

A 156-kilometer multi-use recreational trail on an abandoned railway bed. Perfect for hiking, mountain biking, gravel riding, or bringing your ATV in the backcountry.

Activities: ATV, mountain biking, nature strolls
Terrain: Hard-packed gravel rail trail

Marmora Open-Pit Mine

Geological Wonder • 15 Mins Away

A massive historic open-pit iron ore mine, now filled with striking crystal-clear turquoise groundwater. Stunning panoramic viewing platform off Highway 7.

Pit Depth: Over 700 feet deep
Access: Free viewing platform & parking

Centre Hastings Park

Skate Park & Splash Pad • 8 Mins Away

Ideal for families with young children. Features a world-class concrete skatepark, shaded picnic pavilions, modern splash pad, and playground facilities right in town.

Family Friendly: Free community park & restrooms
Location: Durham St S, Madoc

Camp Supplies & LCBO

Provisions • 7 Mins Away

Stock up your cabin kitchenette easily. Madoc Foodland (full grocery store), local gas stations selling live worms and tackle, and LCBO/The Beer Store are just up the road.

Foodland: Open 7 days a week
Live Bait: Worms, minnows & basic tackle
